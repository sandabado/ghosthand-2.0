# V5 The Gates — focused verification plan

V5 turns the four Craft chambers into real mini-interactions while retaining each chamber's existing `tlPass` dolly. The governing sources are D01–D07 in `RESOLVED-DECISIONS.md`, the organized v2 Craft contracts, and the v5 grimoire roadmap. Green and bone replace amber in Forge; enemy blue and crimson do not belong in any Craft gate. Local snaps, typing, locks, stamp ignition, flight, and particles are allowed under D02 and must consume the shared clock and compositor.

## Deterministic source and math checks

Run `node scripts/verify-gates.mjs` after integration. Check finite normalized board anchors, meaningful source/target separation, Forge's matching-slot and tolerance behavior across desktop/mobile aspect ratios, circular Own hit testing rather than rectangular bounds, bounded deterministic Fleet recognition, defensive rejection of invalid input, terminal seed normalization, finite non-collapsed World transforms, bounded particle attributes, deterministic seeded data, and shared palette/clock ownership. Geometry checks establish CPU invariants; they do not establish cursor tracking, GPU appearance, perceived weight, or human interaction quality.

## Browser acceptance

1. Enter through Threshold → Legend → Drain → Forge using the real journey controls. Confirm no forced state navigation, one live Canvas/composer/heartbeat, and the four gates remain sequentially guarded.
2. Forge: press one loose strand and move it. Confirm the visible endpoint follows the pointer before release. Drop short of the matching slot, on another slot, and outside the board; none seats it. Releasing within the matching tolerance should magnetically settle it and illuminate its structure. Repeat for all three. Observe that completion and Next wait for the final settle. Keyboard alternatives invoke the same seating destination. Repeated input cannot count a strand twice.
3. Train: one or two input characters leave it idle. Three characters start ghost completion; output must visibly develop over shared timeline progress rather than appear complete instantly. The input is treated as text, never evaluated. Check keyboard, paste, composition/IME, and non-BMP characters according to the documented seed rule. Guard against repeated completion and confirm the green compile response precedes Next becoming available.
4. Own: click without travel, release over the source, drop outside the ring, and release inside the ring's bounding box but outside its circle. None locks. A true drag into the ring carries the tool with the cursor, seats it, and ignites 主 through the local lock effect. The same destination is reachable by keyboard. Repeated pointer-up or a pointer-up without an active press cannot complete the gate.
5. Fleet: click, short movement, downward travel, and predominantly sideways travel do not dispatch. A valid upward flick/sweep sends exactly that hand into the dark with its local trail/burst. Three distinct hands are needed; one hand cannot be counted repeatedly. Confirm the final flight settles before completion, with the same route available to keyboard users.
6. Cancel each active gesture through pointer cancellation, lost capture, blur, Escape, and a second pointer. Confirm no delayed completion, stale armed state, or incorrect release after the next interaction. Scrolling outside the board remains ordinary page/panel scrolling.
7. Check desktop, 390px, and 320px widths for horizontal overflow, readable instructions, reachable controls, at least 44px targets, keyboard focus, and the Next action. Use actual browser touch dispatch for an emulated touch pass; name it as emulation rather than physical-device proof.
8. Load with reduced motion and repeat the full four-gate route. The static representation must retain each interactive outcome and all fourteen storyboard states. No delayed animation is required before advancing. Change the preference during a gate's active settle/typing/flight; document and check immediate static settlement of accepted work without inventing completion of unperformed work.
9. Replay and re-enter. Verify all gate accepted/settled arrays, terminal seed/phase, lock state, drag state, and metrics reset coherently; mount counts remain lifecycle-owned. Exiting a gate must dispose its live resources and stop its local rite, including frozen previous-scene overlap behavior.
10. Continue Fleet → Pursuit → Merge → Fork. Confirm only one Pursuit, unchanged D03 hand/sword/flash/cut markers, all-audio silence from 7–8s, interactive Fork at 8s, and the separate shing cap. Craft must not add a full sword moment or consume a shing cue.
11. Audit actual runtime requests and console. Source scans are a separate zero-tracker/closed-palette attestation. A settled native callback sample must still show one recurring shared heartbeat and no independent application timer/RAF.
